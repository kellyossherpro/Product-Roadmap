Meeting notes

1. Retail Crash (top priority)

  • Two POCs on the go (Patrick and Kezio) — need to be brought together; next step is a delivery workshop + architecture.
  • Go big: leverage the Aviator brand, bundle our crash games into a retail crash portfolio, target operators with hundreds of shops (Hollywood, BetKing, Kings) rather than only small Kenyan operators. Africa is the priority region.
  • Retail crash = a hardware device in-shop with 4–6 retail "mice" plugged in; players interact with the crash screen. Retail mouse chosen as the most familiar, flexible, replaceable device.
  • Feron: can't sell without something tangible to see/touch/test — need to know what stage the product is at, then run it through marketing (Patrick) to make it appealing. Feron will have high-level conversations with tier-1 African clients (the "big six/seven") and feed a summarised view back to Product.
  • Key insight: Hollywood may already run an anonymous retail wallet (cash voucher, play in-shop). If true, we integrate into their ecosystem and plug our games/devices in — same play possible with BetKing and Kings. Feron to verify with Wade at Hollywood (apprehensive re SA AML/FIC — KYC threshold ~R4,999; more likely viable in the rest of Africa than SA).
  • Steven: incorporating Aviator into the solution is the surest way to get all the tier ones to take it.
  • BetKing: raised, but low confidence they know their retail market (Alvaro is online-focused; Adeola/Nigeria team now drive retail). Kings could work as a simple Betman voucher sell-point over the counter.
  • Watch-out: crash retail is a cross-team dev effort and could drain resources from other work.

2. Full portfolio into Betman Retail (retail wallet)

  • Bring the whole lobby into the retail wallet — bet on scheduled and instant games (ours + third-party like Aviator) via a cashier voucher on terminals or mobile in-shop.
  • Makes us a one-stop shop for retail operators (scheduled + instant), matching how Africa delivers instant games (terminals/PC/tablet/mobile). Fundamentally changes in-shop betting — shorter queues, multiple bets against one wallet balance without returning to the counter.
  • Needs commercial to understand and quantify the value, then prioritise. Dependency: BetSupport v6.

3. Instant Games — pick two

  • Instant Racing — 6-runner, static odds, full VOD 3D animation, greyhounds + horses, easy Quinella/Trifecta/Exacta. Seen as the no-brainer differentiator: global appeal, stands out on the casino book, leverages our assets. Steven: a notable omission from our portfolio.
  • Hop — POC seen; addictive, pure-bet (no window duration); the underlying maths opens many game types (penalty shootout, golf, etc.). Works retail and online (single-player hardware constraint). Under technical review by Ravi — possible blockers. IP-sensitive — do NOT mention Hop/Harp to customers.
  • Cricket roulette — POC exists; only viable if the Stake.com deal lands (no cricket customer currently).
  • Others — instant Legend Shootout (make scheduled version instant); instant Animalitos (back burner, no success yet); instant Goal (need Goal success first). Crash: Thunderbird & Midnight Unicorn coming → portfolio of 3, heading to ~5. Astro → Betman already in process.
  • Process tension (Paul): wants RICE scoring (reach / value / effort), but RICE needs commercial insight, which needs a prototype — chicken-and-egg. All these games take roughly the same dev time. Agreed path: socialise the roadmap → gather commercial feedback → feed RICE. Customer research is fine on variations of existing scheduled games, but not on Hop.

4. Scheduled games

  • Goal Play (was "Goal") — virtual football condensed to a 3-goal sequence with lottery-style any-order combination markets across 6 goal events (right foot, left foot, header, penalty, miss, save), plus the main match-result markets. Rides the micro-event betting trend. Good early feedback from Italian customers (CISOL, GBO); BetKing (Alvaro) hesitant. Target: launch this year. Feron flagged the name/logo (the "GOOOL" 3-O logo reads as "go pay/go play") — Patrick to weigh in.
  • Fast racing / faster football — sub-one-minute horse racing, quicker football, modernised racing screens, improved cinematic finish-line. Goal: move all online products to 1-minute game intervals (Rob: Entain's 1-minute virtuals drive most of their income). Goal Play suits this well.
  • American-football Goal game — quarters with 3 scoring events; awaiting evidence, prove Goal Play first. Goal-version needs ~10× fewer animation clips than a full AmFootball virtual.
  • Multiplier feature — selling to BetKing (interested, slow) and CECL/Turkey (interested, stalled over summer); needs a commercial push.
  • Promo tools (item 9) — freebets, leaderboards, jackpots (leverage jackpots more). Commercial-driven; overdue.

5. Vision X

  • Adding racing to the Vision X portfolio (alongside instant racing) — an easy build once instant racing is done.
  • Under-embraced commercially despite carrying ~98% of our revenue-generating products (Kino, football — multicast + localised commentary, Spin & Win, number games, Lucky Loot). Net4Media/Raspberry Pi lets operators control which games show on which screens; football preambles added so a shop can run entirely off Vision X.
  • Steven: are AMs/sales confident on Vision X USPs vs Golden Race? May need refresher training — Rob & Sarah to re-check their teams; Sarah to look at rollout blockers.
  • Feron: Gerald & Cindy cite game availability as a blocker; Product's view is everything is already there → set up a Vision X portfolio review session with AMs/sales (Josh to arrange, send attendee list).
